🐟 Meet Your Fish

🐠
Sand bass swim in giant schools — sometimes hundreds of fish together! Find one and you've found a BUNCH.
💦
They chase tiny fish called shad up to the top of the water. Sometimes the water looks like it's boiling — that means fish are right there!
🐦
Watch for birds diving into the water. Birds are eating the shad too — so there are always fish right underneath them. Birds = fish!
💪
They're little but super strong — they'll pull and tug on your line and make your rod bend. So fun!
🏆
Most are about as big as your hand or a little bigger — and you're allowed to catch lots of them!

🎣 How We'll Catch Them

1
Find the fish. Dad watches the fish-finder screen and looks for birds. When we see a big group, we stop!
2
Drop it down. Let your shiny lure sink way down to where the fish are. Then lift it up and down — jiggle, jiggle!
3
Watch the bobber. If we use a bobber, keep your eyes on it. When it goes UNDER — reel, reel, reel!
4
Fish on! Hold tight, keep your rod up, and wind the handle. Dad will help scoop it with the net. 🥳
5
High five! Say hi to your fish, take a picture, and drop it in the cooler or let it swim. Then do it again!
